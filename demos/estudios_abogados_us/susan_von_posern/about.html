About

Susan von Posern — immigration attorney you can trust.

For over three decades, Susan von Posern has practiced immigration law the same way from the start: personally, carefully, and in her clients' language.

Susan von Posern earned her Juris Doctor from Emory Law School in 1993 and was admitted to the California State Bar that same year — more than 32 years of active practice. She earned her Bachelor of Arts in Foreign Languages from Emory University in 1990.

After years in general practice, Susan chose to dedicate her work exclusively to immigration law in 2003. For her, immigration is not one practice area among many — it is the entire practice.

Her model is deliberately boutique. Susan handles each case herself — one attorney, one client, from the first consultation to the final decision. No hand-offs, no anonymous teams.

Fully bilingual in English and Spanish, she prepares every matter with the care her clients deserve — and explains it in plain language along the way.

“No case will ever be lost because of poor preparation, poor communication, or lack of knowledge of the law.”
